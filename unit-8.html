What this record is

  • It is kept in this browser, on this device, under the name you signed in with.
  • It does not follow you to another device on its own. Use the transfer code, or just send your results through the form, which is the copy your tutor keeps.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere until you press a send button.
  • Clearing your browser data clears it. Send your results before you do that.
  • Several people can share one device. Each name keeps a separate record.

For the tutor

Set this up once for the whole unit

One form covers Unit 8. Every criterion writes into the same response workbook, which is what makes the tracker link work.

1. Build the form

In the class team, add a Forms tab. Name it Unit 8 games record. Six questions:

No.QuestionTypeSettings
1Your nameText, shortRequired
2Which gameChoiceG1 to G15, one option each. Required
3Your best scoreText, shortRequired. Format is 11 of 12
4How many times you played itText, shortRequired
5Verification codeText, shortRequired. The hub supplies it
6Anything you want me to go overText, longNot required

Settings: people in my organisation, record name on, one response per person off, show results automatically off. Nothing here is scored.

2. Get the prefill link

  1. Open the form, press Collect responses, then Get a link to prefill answers.
  2. Type a marker into each box: put NAMEHERE in question 1, GAMEHERE in 2, SCOREHERE in 3, PLAYSHERE in 4, CODEHERE in 5.
  3. Press Get link, copy it, and paste it below.

Do this in Notepad, not here

Pasting the link on this page saves it to this device only. A learner who downloads the file gets a copy with nothing in it.

Right click the file, Open with, Notepad. Press Ctrl and F and search for TUTOR SETTINGS. It is about thirty lines down. You will find two lines there, one beginning var FORM_PREFILL and one beginning var CLASS_LIST. Each ends with a pair of empty quote marks.

Paste your prefill link between the quote marks on the first line. Put the class names between the quote marks on the second, separated by a vertical bar. Save, and upload that copy to the class team. Every learner who downloads it then has both.

Each of those two lines appears once in the whole file, so a find and replace cannot go wrong.

The box below is still useful for testing the link before you paste it into the file.

The hub swaps those markers for the learner's real values. Saving here covers this device only. Editing the file in Notepad covers everybody.

3. Link the responses to the tracker

  1. In Forms, press Open results in Excel. That workbook lives on your OneDrive and updates itself.
  2. Open the tracker, Data, Get Data, From File, From Workbook, and point it at that file.
  3. Load it onto the Paste here sheet. The Games record sheet and the class overview fill themselves.
  4. Press Refresh All whenever you want the latest submissions.

4. The class list

Paste the names, one per line. They then appear as buttons on the sign in page on this device.

What this cannot do

  • A browser cannot see another device. The hub only knows what was played where it is open.
  • A learner's record does not follow them from one device to another. They can move it with the transfer code on their My record page, but the form is the copy that reaches you.
  • The verification code proves the score came from the hub. It does not prove who played.
  • A score is a score. It does not tell you whether a learner understood anything.
